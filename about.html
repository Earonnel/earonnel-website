Where I’m coming from

My name is Prince Earonnel Del Mundo Arroyo, and I’m studying accountancy at the University of Santo Tomas’ AMV College of Accountancy in the Philippines. I’m interested in how accounting, business, and clear analysis help people understand choices and make decisions.

My academic work has included collaborative projects and a school research project about financial literacy and budgeting among senior high school students. I’m also interested in student leadership, career development, and the kind of practical skills that grow through working with other people.

Life outside the classroom

Cycling and running are important interests outside academics. They give this website another dimension beyond school: routes, training, photos, things I learn, and the experience of staying active while balancing other commitments.

I want this website to be a useful record of the whole picture rather than a static résumé. It will grow as I write, finish projects, and have experiences worth documenting.

Why EARONNEL?

EARONNEL is the name I want this personal corner of the internet to be built around. The site uses my full name, Prince Earonnel Del Mundo Arroyo, where identity and context matter, while “EARONNEL.” acts as the simple visual brand.

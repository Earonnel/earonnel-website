THE APPROACH

Understand first. Then explain.

This page is intended for notes and resources I create or meaningfully synthesize. It is not a dump of private class materials.

Formats: written explainers, diagrams, short videos, and study reflections.

Accountancy & reporting

Topics from accounting coursework can be turned into clear, original notes: the question being solved, the underlying principle, a worked example, and common mistakes to watch for.

Business & decision-making

Business analysis, economics, forecasting, and quantitative decision-making are all areas that can benefit from step-by-step explanations. When an example comes from a class assignment, it should be checked for sharing permissions before publishing.

Financial literacy

Financial literacy and budgeting have been part of my school research interests. This space can collect general educational notes about budgeting concepts and ways to think about spending — clearly separated from personal financial advice.

Learning systems

I’m interested in the process as well as the subject: how to break down a difficult topic, practice actively, find mistakes, and explain an idea simply. I’ll share what has genuinely helped me rather than claim one system works for everyone.

This section is a foundation, not a claim that every topic already has a published article. As I add posts, each should have a clear title, examples, sources where needed, and a date of publication.

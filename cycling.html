RIDE JOURNAL

Routes, moments, notes.

Use each entry to record the date, route, distance if known, conditions, photos, and what stood out. Publish only details you are comfortable making public.

Why I want to document rides

Cycling is one of the interests that belongs in this website alongside school and projects. Ride journals can preserve more than metrics: the route, the scenery, a lesson from the day, and the reason a particular ride was memorable.

What a ride entry can include

  • Route: a general route or area, without disclosing sensitive routines or precise real-time location.
  • Ride details: date, distance, moving time, or elevation if you have verified data you want to share.
  • Photos: a small gallery with captions and alt text.
  • Reflection: what you enjoyed, what you would change, and what you learned.
  • Gear notes: products you actually used, with honest context and no invented reviews.

Photo journals and ride videos

This page is ready for photo essays and embedded videos. A post can combine a short written story, a few selected images, and a video if one exists. There are no invented routes or ride statistics on this starter page; future entries should be based on your own rides.

Privacy tip: publish ride information after the ride, not live. Avoid showing your home, regular start point, or a predictable real-time route if that could compromise your safety.

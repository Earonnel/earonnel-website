A NOTE FROM EARONNEL

Why I’m building EARONNEL.

I wanted one place on the internet that could grow alongside me — not just a résumé, and not just another social profile.

PERSONAL NOTE · PRINCIPLES, NOT A FINISHED STORY

My name is Prince Earonnel Del Mundo Arroyo. I’m an accountancy student, and I have interests that reach beyond school: business, learning, cycling, running, and the projects I’m figuring out along the way.

I wanted a website that could hold all of those things together. A place where someone who searches my full name can find a clear, accurate introduction — and where I can keep adding useful work as I learn and grow.

More than an online résumé

A résumé is designed to summarize experience. That matters, but it doesn’t always leave room for the process: the notes that helped a concept click, a school project that taught me something, a ride worth remembering, or a small lesson from training.

I want this site to make space for that process. Some things might become articles. Others might be photo journals, short videos, or project summaries. I don’t need to have a huge archive on day one. I just need a good foundation and a reason to keep updating it.

A place that can change with me

Right now, I’m balancing accountancy and business-related learning with interests outside the classroom. As those interests develop, the website can change too. The important thing is that what I publish should be real, useful, and clearly described — not an inflated version of who I am.

That’s the idea behind EARONNEL.: a personal corner of the internet for the work, the movement, the questions, and the progress in between.

Thanks for stopping by.
Prince Earonnel Del Mundo Arroyo

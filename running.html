RUN JOURNAL

Progress, not performance theatre.

A log can be useful without turning every run into a personal record. Honest notes are more valuable than made-up metrics.

A place for the process

Running is one of my interests outside academics. This page is a space to document how it fits into the rest of life — the sessions, the routines, the goals, and the lessons that come from being consistent over time.

What I can track

  • Run notes: date, distance, time, and effort if recorded and verified.
  • Training reflections: what felt good, what was difficult, and what I want to adjust next time.
  • Milestones: personal goals or events once they genuinely happen.
  • Photos and video: occasional visual snapshots of the journey.

Keeping it balanced

This website is about a whole life, not an endless scoreboard. Training updates should fit around school, rest, and other responsibilities. I won't publish performance claims or personal bests unless they're real and recorded accurately.

This is a starter page for future entries, not a claim that a specific training plan, race, or result has already happened.
